## 1. Action Plan for Core Conjecture 1: The Cohomological Vanishing Conjecture for Choice Residuums (`conj:cohom-vanishing`, Issue #8)

### 1.1 Mathematical Audit & Scoping Summary (`docs/categorical_reverse_mathematics.tex#L1022`)
- **Statement Audited:** Conjecture `\ref{conj:cohom-vanishing}` (Chapter 2, §2.4, Lines 1022–1030; restated in Chapter 7, §7.3, Line 2727) posits for each weak choice axiom $A$ a canonical slice-indexed assignment $Y \mapsto \mathcal{G}_A(Y) \subseteq \mathbf{Grp}(\mathcal{E}/Y)$ of internal structure groups in any Grothendieck topos $\mathcal{E}$ (with an $\mathrm{NNO}$) such that $(A \Rightarrow_{\mathbf{Topos}} \mathrm{AC})$ is equivalent to internal Booleanness ($\Omega_{\mathcal{E}} \simeq 1+1$), external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$, and $H^1(\mathcal{E}/Y, G) = 0$ for all $Y \in \mathcal{E}$ and all $G \in \mathcal{G}_A(Y)$.
- **Audit Rectifications Executed in Manuscript:**
  1. **Unconditional Proof of Baseline $A = \top$ (Line 1036):** Proved in full generality across any Grothendieck topos $\mathcal{E}$ with an $\mathrm{NNO}$ via a two-stage torsor reduction extending Blass's discrete $\mathrm{ZF}$ theorem (`[Blass83]`, *Trans. Amer. Math. Soc.* **279** (1983), 257–269) without invoking well-foundedness or Hartogs's ordinal:
     - *Stage 1 (Abelian Augmentation Torsor — Internal Multiple Choice):* In the Boolean slice $\mathcal{E}' := \mathcal{E}/Y$, every object $X$ has decidable equality, so the Kuratowski-finitely supported functions $\mathbb{Z}^{(X)} \rightarrowtail \mathbb{Z}^X$ carry decidable, cardinal-finite supports $\mathrm{supp}(f) \in K(X)$ (`[Johnstone02]`, §D5.2 & §D5.4). For any epimorphism $e : X \twoheadrightarrow Y$, the summation map $\varepsilon : \mathbb{Z}^{(X)} \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{Z}_{\mathcal{E}'}$ ($\delta_x \mapsto 1$) is epic with abelian kernel $K_X := \ker(\varepsilon) \in \mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{E}')$. Vanishing of $H^1(\mathcal{E}/Y, K_X)$ splits the $K_X$-torsor $\varepsilon^{-1}(1) \twoheadrightarrow 1_{\mathcal{E}'}$, yielding $\sigma$ whose support $F := \mathrm{supp}(\sigma) \rightarrowtail X$ is inhabited ($\varepsilon(\sigma) = 1$) of unique internal cardinal $c : 1_{\mathcal{E}'} \to \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$.
     - *Stage 2 (Non-Abelian Finite-Frame Torsor — Single-Element Selection):* The internal bijection object $\underline{\mathrm{Iso}}_{\mathcal{E}'}([c], F) \twoheadrightarrow 1_{\mathcal{E}'}$ is a principal torsor under $\underline{\mathrm{Aut}}_{\mathcal{E}'}([c]) \in \mathbf{Grp}(\mathcal{E}')$; vanishing of $H^1(\mathcal{E}/Y, \underline{\mathrm{Aut}}_{\mathcal{E}'}([c]))$ supplies $\varphi$, and evaluating $s := \varphi(0) : Y \to F \rightarrowtail X$ splits $e$.
     - *Role of External Projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$:* At $A = \top$, vanishing over $Y = 1_{\mathcal{E}}$ already forces external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$, whereas for stronger internal axioms up to $A = \mathrm{IAC}$ ($\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{IAC}}(Y) = \emptyset$), external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$ is strictly independent and indispensable, witnessed by $\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}}$ (`thm:iac-to-ac`, Guardrail 5).
  2. **Slice-Indexing Bug Fix in Chapter 2 Summary Table (Lines 1117 & 1120):** Rectified $H^1(\mathcal{E}, G) = 0$ to $H^1(\mathcal{E}/Y, G) = 0$. In Freyd's Boolean two-valued non-localic countermodel $\mathcal{F} = \mathbf{Sh}_{\neg\neg}(\mathbb{A})$ (`[Freyd72]`), $1_{\mathcal{F}}$ is externally projective—so $H^1(\mathcal{F}, G) = 0$ over $Y = 1_{\mathcal{F}}$ for all $G$—yet $\mathcal{F} \not\models \mathrm{AC}$ because non-trivial torsor obstructions persist over non-terminal slices $\mathcal{F}/Y$.
  3. **Explicit Conjectural Framing Across Prose and Tables (Lines 1032, 1122, 2727):** Tagged remaining open choice-spectrum hypotheses ($\mathrm{WISC}$, $\mathrm{OP}$, $\mathrm{AC}_\lambda$, and Chapter 2 Summary Table conjectural cells) cleanly with `(Conj.~\ref{...})`.
  4. **Unconditional Reduction of $A = \mathrm{IBPI}$ Completed (Line 1038, Issue #56):** Rectified bare $\Omega$-compactness to $\mathrm{IBPI}$ (internal ultrafilters on non-trivial Boolean algebras $B \in \mathbf{BA}(\mathcal{E})$) plus $\Omega_{\mathcal{E}} \simeq 1+1$ and external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$; proved via quotient Boolean algebras $B = A/I \subseteq 2^{2^F}/I$ and Kuratowski induction (`[Johnstone02]`, §D5.4) that all fiberwise Kuratowski-finite epimorphisms and cofiltered profinite towers split globally, reducing $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{IBPI}}(Y)$ unconditionally to discrete torsion-free abelian augmentation kernels $K_X = \ker(\mathbb{Z}^{(X)} \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{Z}_{\mathcal{E}/Y}) \in \mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{E}/Y)$.
  5. **Unconditional Base & Tower Reduction of $A = \mathrm{IDC}$ & Counterexample Refutation Completed (Line 1040, Issue #57):** Proved unconditionally under $\mathrm{IDC}$, $\Omega_{\mathcal{E}} \simeq 1+1$, and external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$ that least-number minimization (`[Johnstone02]`, §D5.1) and prefix-section $\omega$-towers kill $H^1(\mathcal{E}/Y, G)$ over internally subcountable codomains $Y$ ($\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{IDC}}(Y) = \emptyset$) and kill sequential $\varprojlim^1$ obstructions along $\omega$-towers with $H^1$-vanishing kernels (`thm:limit-residuum`), reducing $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{IDC}}(Y)$ via $A = \top$ to $K_X$ and $\underline{\mathrm{Aut}}_{\mathcal{E}/Y}([c])$ over internally uncountable codomains $Y$; refuted restricting fiber groups to uncountably presented groups via the non-split $\Delta(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})$-torsor $2^{\mathbb{N}}/E_0^{\mathrm{even}} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{Q}_{E_0}$ (`rem:pauli-z-parity`, `[Serafin23]`) in $\mathrm{ZF} + \mathrm{DC} + \mathrm{BP}$.
  6. **Unconditional Single-Slice Reductions of $A = \mathrm{SVC}_{\mathrm{ret}}$ and Bare $\mathrm{SVC}$ Completed (Line 1042, Issue #58):** Proved unconditionally that retract-bounded $\mathrm{SVC}_{\mathrm{ret}}(S)$ over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ collapses $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{SVC}_{\mathrm{ret}}}$ to the single slice $Y = S$, whereas for Blass's bare subquotient $\mathrm{SVC}(S)$ (`[Blass79]`) over any base $\mathbf{Set}$ (where the quotient fallacy blocks $Y = S$ alone), shifting the single test slice to $Y = P^+(S) \rightarrowtail 2^S$ yields internal well-ordering of $S$ by Zermelo's theorem (`[Johnstone02]`, §D4.5) and complete-frame induction on $\Delta\kappa \times S$, splitting every epimorphism (separated from $1_{\mathcal{E}}$-projectivity and checked on $\{0,1\} \in P^+(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})$ via $\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}}$)—completing all four benchmark strata of `conj:cohom-vanishing` while scoping remaining open work on intermediate axioms ($\mathrm{WISC}$, $\mathrm{OP}$, Kihara's $\mathrm{AC}_\lambda$).

## 2. Action Plan for Core Conjecture 3: The Zero-Dimensionality Residuum Conjecture (`conj:bpi-ac`, Issue #6)

### 2.1 Mathematical Audit & Scoping Rectifications (`docs/categorical_reverse_mathematics.tex#L954-971`)
- **Statement Audited:** Conjecture `\ref{conj:bpi-ac}` (Chapter 2, §2.3.2, Lines 954–971; introduced in Chapter 1, Line 722 and Chapter 2, Line 859; restated in Chapter 2 Summary Table, Line 1120, Chapter 7, §7.3, Line 2731, and Chapter 7 Master Reference Atlas Row 5, Line 2852). It originally formulated an un-scoped equivalence in an arbitrary coherent topos $\mathcal{E}$ between $\mathrm{IBPI} + (\Omega_{\mathcal{E}} \simeq 1+1) + \text{Internal Zero-Dimensionality}$ and $\mathrm{IAC}$, glossing $\mathrm{IBPI}$ as "logical compactness of $\Omega_{\mathcal{E}}$" and defining internal zero-dimensionality on arbitrary open/coherent covers.
- **Four Structural Defects Identified and Rectified:**
  1. **Localic Collapse Over Weakened Bases (`L956`, `L960`):** Over *any* base topos $\mathcal{S} \models \mathrm{BPI}$, a Boolean coherent localic topos $\mathbf{Sh}_{\mathcal{S}}(L)$ must collapse to a finite power $\mathcal{S}^m$: if $\mathcal{O}(L)$ possessed an atomless summand $c > 0$, it would carry an internal ultrafilter $u \subseteq \mathcal{O}(L){\downarrow}c$ whose complements $\{c \wedge \neg x : x \in u\}$ form a directed join to $c$ admitting no finite subcover, contradicting coherent compactness of $1_L$; thus $L$ is finite atomic ($1_L = a_1 \vee \dots \vee a_m$). Consequently $\mathbf{Sh}_{\mathcal{S}}(L) \simeq \mathcal{S}^m$ automatically satisfies internal zero-dimensionality, yet satisfies $\mathrm{IAC}$ if and only if $\mathcal{S} \models \mathrm{IAC}$. Over Halpern–Lévy bases $\mathcal{S} \models \mathrm{ZF} + \mathrm{BPI} + \neg\mathrm{AC}$, the original un-scoped biconditional fails outright. Scoping `conj:bpi-ac` to coherent Grothendieck topoi $\mathcal{E}$ over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ (or over a Boolean base $\mathcal{S} \models \mathrm{IAC}$) eliminates this counterexample while retaining external $\mathrm{AC}$ lift via external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$.
  2. **Subobject-Cover Triviality vs. Groupoid-Twisted Epimorphic Covers (`L956`, `L969`):** In *any* Boolean topos over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$, every finite cover by *subobjects* $\{U_i \rightarrowtail Y\}_{i=1}^n$ trivially refines to a mutually disjoint clopen partition $V_i := U_i \wedge \neg \bigvee_{j < i} U_j$ (`[Johnstone02]`, §D4.5). Yet Boolean coherent non-localic topoi over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ can satisfy $\mathrm{IBPI}$ and $\Omega_{\mathcal{E}} \simeq 1+1$ while failing $\mathrm{IAC}$—for example Halpern's oligomorphic atomic permutation topos $\mathbf{Cont}(\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{T}))$ (`[Caramello18]`, `[Kihara26]`) or finite-group $\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}}$. The real pasting obstruction therefore lives on **non-monic** finite coherent epimorphic covers $\{u_i : U_i \to Y\}_{i=1}^n$ ($\coprod_{i=1}^n U_i \twoheadrightarrow Y$) whose fiberwise isotropy twists local sections.
  3. **Rectified Étale Internal Zero-Dimensionality & Proved Necessity (`L964`, `L969`):** Defining *étale internal zero-dimensionality* as the existence of an inhabited cover $E \twoheadrightarrow 1_{\mathcal{E}}$ such that in $\mathcal{E}/E$, every finite coherent epimorphic family $\{u_i : U_i \to Y\}_{i=1}^n$ refines to a finite complemented subobject partition $Y \cong \coprod_{j=1}^m V_j$ factoring through sections $V_j \to U_{i(j)}$, **necessity** ($\mathrm{IAC} \implies$ étale internal zero-dimensionality) is unconditionally proved at $E = 1_{\mathcal{E}}$ by taking a global section $s : Y \to \coprod_{i=1}^n U_i$ and pulling back the coproduct injections $V_i := s^*(\iota_i)$.
  4. **Precise Characterization of $\mathrm{IBPI}$ & Master Atlas Tagging (`L722`, `L859`, `L954`, `L1120`, `L2731`, `L2852`):** Rectified "logical compactness of $\Omega_{\mathcal{E}}$" across all occurrences to "every non-trivial internal Boolean algebra $B \in \mathbf{BA}(\mathcal{E})$ admits an ultrafilter (equivalently internal compactness of Stone/coherent locales, `[Johnstone82]`, `[Johnstone02]`)," and tagged Ch. 7 Master Reference Atlas Row 5 (`L2852`) explicitly with `(Conj.~\ref{conj:bpi-ac})`.

## 3. Action Plan for Core Conjecture 2: The Internal $\omega$-Presentability Residuum Conjecture (`conj:dc-ac`, Issue #7)

- **Statement Audited:** Conjecture `\ref{conj:dc-ac}` (Chapter 2, §2.3.3, Lines 977–989; Chapter 1, Lines 724–730; Chapter 2 Table, Line 1121; Chapter 7, §7.3, Line 2729; Chapter 7 Master Reference Atlas Row 6, Line 2853) originally conjectured inside any Boolean elementary or Grothendieck topos $\mathcal{E}$ with an $\mathrm{NNO}$ that $\mathrm{IDC} + (\text{every epimorphism } e : X \twoheadrightarrow Y \text{ is a retract in } \mathcal{E}/Y \text{ of } \varprojlim_{n\in\omega} X_n \twoheadrightarrow Y \text{ with decidable Kuratowski-finite fibers}) \iff \mathrm{IAC}$.
- **Structural Refutations & Proved Partial Benchmarks Executed in Manuscript:**
  1. **Bidirectional Refutation of Finite-Fiber $\omega$-Tower Retracts (`L977`):**
     - *Necessity Fails Even in $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{ZFC}} \models \mathrm{IAC}$:* Any inverse limit $\varprojlim_{n\in\omega} X_n \twoheadrightarrow 1$ with finite fibers has cardinality at most $\aleph_0^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0}$, so any $X$ of cardinality $> 2^{\aleph_0}$ cannot be a retract (nor quotient) of such a finite-fiber $\omega$-tower.
     - *Sufficiency Fails on $2$-to-$1$ Covers Under $\mathrm{IDC}$ Over Uncountable Codomains:* Even $2$-to-$1$ finite-fiber covers fail to split globally or internally under $\mathrm{IDC}$, both in $\mathrm{ZF}+\mathrm{DC}+\mathrm{BP}$ on the Pauli-$Z$ parity cover $2^{\mathbb{N}}/E_0^{\mathrm{even}} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{Q}_{E_0}$ (`rem:pauli-z-parity`, `[Serafin23]`) and over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ in Kihara's Rung 1–2 atomic topoi $\mathbf{Sh}(\mathcal{K}_\lambda^{\mathrm{op}}, J_{\mathrm{at}})$ at $\lambda^{<\lambda} = \lambda > \aleph_0$ (`[Kihara26]`) via unordered-pair quotient covers $V^{(2)} \twoheadrightarrow \binom{V}{2}$ of generic sets of size $\lambda^+$.
  2. **Unconditional Epimorphism-Level Subcountable-Step Theorem & Profinite $2$-Adic Separation (`L977`):**
     - In any Boolean topos $\mathcal{E}$ with an $\mathrm{NNO}$, least-number minimization (`[Johnstone02]`, §D5.1) and prefix-section chains prove unconditionally that $\mathrm{IDC}$ combined with *étale forward refinement* ($E^* e \circ r = \pi_0$ along an inhabited cover $E \twoheadrightarrow 1_{\mathcal{E}}$) over pullbacks of subcountable-codomain epimorphisms $q_n : A_n \twoheadrightarrow B_n$ ($B_n \rightarrowtail \mathbb{N}_{\mathcal{E}/E}$) is equivalent to $\mathrm{IAC}$.
     - *Non-Tautologicality Without $\mathrm{IDC}$ (`\mathbf{Cont}(\mathbb{Z}_2)` vs. `\mathbf{Nom}`):* In profinite $\mathbf{Cont}(\mathbb{Z}_2)$, every epimorphism $e : X \twoheadrightarrow Y$ admits an equivariant refinement from a countable disjoint union $\coprod_{m \in \mathbb{N}} (\mathbb{Z}_2 / 2^m\mathbb{Z}_2) \twoheadrightarrow \Delta\mathbb{N}$ via $r_i(g, h) = (h + ((g - h) \bmod 2^{a_i})) \cdot x_i$, whereas $\mathrm{IDC}$ fails in $\mathbf{Cont}(\mathbb{Z}_2)$ (and in $\mathbf{Nom}$, where finite-support transpositions obstruct countable refinement of $\mathbb{A}^{\#(2)} \twoheadrightarrow \binom{\mathbb{A}}{2}$).
  3. **Rectified Intrinsic Conjecture Over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ (`L981–987`), Boolean-Étendue Necessity, Normal Pro-Discrete Collapse, & Master Atlas Tagging (`L1121`, `L2729`, `L2853`):**
     - Scoped `conj:dc-ac` to Boolean Grothendieck topoi $\mathcal{E}$ with an $\mathrm{NNO}$ over $\mathbf{Set}_{\mathrm{AC}}$ (or $\mathcal{S} \models \mathrm{IAC}$, contrasting external base-$\mathrm{DC}$ $\mathrm{CCC}$ locales in `thm:bdf-deformed`) via **étale $\omega$-presentability of localic groupoid isotropy**: $\mathcal{E}$ admits a Joyal–Tierney open localic groupoid presentation $\mathbb{G} = (G_1 \rightrightarrows G_0)$ (`[Johnstone02]`, §C5.3) whose identity section $e(G_0) \subseteq G_1$ is an $\omega$-cofinal intersection of open subgroupoid neighborhoods over $G_0$.
     - Proved **necessity** unconditionally on Boolean étendues (`[Johnstone02]`, §D4.5 / Freyd–Scedrov: $e(G_0) \subseteq G_1$ is clopen), proved **sufficiency** on normal-basis pro-discrete permutation topoi $\mathbf{Cont}(\mathcal{G})$ ($\bigcap_{n \in \omega} g_n U_n g_n^{-1} = \bigcap_{n \in \omega} U_n = \{1_{\mathcal{G}}\}$ open, forcing $\mathcal{G}$ discrete), and tagged Ch. 7 Master Reference Atlas Row 6 (`L2853`) with `(Conj.~\ref{conj:dc-ac})`.

### 10.2 Algebraic Shadow, the Living Zone, Hypergraph Transversals, & Split-Polarity Transport
1. **Algebraic Shadow & $S4$ Interior (`HomotopyCRM.inquisitor_impossibility_casuistry`):**
   - Passing to the Galois lattice $\mathcal{L}(\mathfrak{P})$ equips a product-closed $Q$-context $(D, \sqcap, \top)$ with a stability monoid $\Sigma \le \mathrm{ProductEndo}(D)$ generating the $S4$ modal interior operator $\Box_\Sigma(T) = \{X \in D \mid \forall \sigma \in \Sigma,\; \sigma(X) \in T\}$ on any product-closed target upper-bound class $T \subseteq D$. Because $\Box_\Sigma$ preserves product closure and captures every $\Sigma$-invariant subclass $\mathcal{U} \subseteq T$, purely negative stability rules cannot avert Dogma without a positive probe suite $\mathcal{P}$.
2. **The Living Zone, Compactness, & Minimal Transversals:**
   - Denote $c(-) := \mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}(-)$ (`mooreClosureOperator`). Given a hypothesis extent $H \subseteq \mathbb{D}$ (`hyp`) and conclusion extent $C \subseteq \mathbb{D}$ (`concl`) with $H \not\subseteq C$, let $T := (H \Rightarrow C) := (\mathbb{D} \setminus H) \cup C$ be the material-implication target ($H \cap E \subseteq C \iff E \subseteq T$) and let $K \subseteq H \cap C$ be a non-empty non-vacuity core (typically $K = H \cap C$, e.g.\ $\{\mathbf{Set}\}$). Non-degenerate residuum discovery inhabits strictly the **Living Zone**:
     $$H \not\subseteq C \;\;(\text{i.e. } T \subsetneq \mathbb{D}), \qquad \mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}(K) \subseteq T \;\;(\text{a fortiori } \{E \in \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{P}) \mid K \subseteq E \subseteq T\} \neq \emptyset), \qquad T \subsetneq \mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}(T)$$
     (neither a vacuous gap $H \subseteq C$, nor Blindness where $\mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}(K)$ fails to separate $K$ from $H \setminus C$ or the resolving frontier is empty, nor Dogma where $T = \mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}(T)$ collapses $\mathrm{Res}_{\mathcal{P}}^{\min}(H \Rightarrow C) = \{T\}$ rather than discovering genuine structural extents $E \subsetneq T$).
   - **Algebraic / Finite-Degree Compactness:** When $\mathcal{P}$ consists of finitary (finite-degree) probes so that $\mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}$ is Scott-continuous (preserving directed unions), ascending chains $E_0 \subseteq E_1 \subseteq \cdots \subseteq T$ of resolving closed extents (dually, descending chains of deductive strength bounded above by $T$) satisfy $\mathrm{Cl}_{\mathcal{P}}(\bigcup_i E_i) = \bigcup_i E_i \subseteq T$, ensuring by Zorn's Lemma that every resolving closed class extends to at least one maximal Pareto element in $\mathrm{Res}_{\mathcal{P}}^{\min}(H \Rightarrow C)$.
   - **Hypergraph Duality ($\mathcal{H}_{H, W}$ vs. $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Her}}$):** Let $W := H \setminus C = \mathbb{D} \setminus T$ be the separating defect witnesses. Define the **Obstruction Hypergraph** $\mathcal{H}_{H, W}$ whose vertices are probes $P \in \mathcal{P}$ and whose hyperedges are the refuting probe sets $\{P \in \mathcal{P} \mid w \not\models P\}$ for minimal separating witnesses $w \in W$ (a probe set $Q$ resolves $H \cap \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{P}}(Q) \subseteq C$ iff $Q$ hits every hyperedge), and the $\perp$-dual **Heresy Hypergraph** $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Her}}$ on witness collections. Minimal hitting sets (hypergraph transversals) $Q \in \mathrm{Tr}(\mathcal{H}_{H, W})$ induce the inclusion-maximal resolving closed extents, yielding $\mathrm{Res}_{\mathcal{P}}^{\min}(H \Rightarrow C) = \max_{\subseteq}\{\mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{P}}(Q) \mid Q \in \mathrm{Tr}(\mathcal{H}_{H, W})\}$.
   - **The $4 \times 2$ Schism Atom (`TestTopos` / `ChoiceProbe`):** Specializing to the 4-object suite $\{\mathbf{Set}, \mathbf{Set}^{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}}, \mathbf{Set}^{\mathcal{M}_1}, \mathbf{Set}^{V^{\mathrm{op}}}\}$ with $H = \mathrm{Ext}(\{\mathrm{IAC}\})$, $C = \mathbf{Mod}(\mathrm{AC}) = \{\mathbf{Set}\}$ (`choiceConcl_ne_extProj` separating $C$ from $\mathrm{Ext}(\{\mathrm{Proj}_{\mathrm{sub}}\})$), $K = H \cap C = \{\mathbf{Set}\}$, and $T = \{\mathbf{Set}, \mathbf{Set}^{\mathcal{M}_1}, \mathbf{Set}^{V^{\mathrm{op}}}\}$ leaves the single separating witness $W = \{\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}}\}$; against $\{\mathrm{Proj}_{\mathrm{sub}}, \mathrm{Localic}\}$, the $4 \times 2$ incidence matrix induces the single hyperedge $\{\mathrm{Proj}_{\mathrm{sub}}, \mathrm{Localic}\}$ whose minimal transversals are precisely $\{\{\mathrm{Proj}_{\mathrm{sub}}\}, \{\mathrm{Localic}\}\}$, realizing $\mathrm{Res}_{\mathcal{P}}^{\min}(H \Rightarrow C) = \{\mathrm{Ext}(\{\mathrm{Proj}_{\mathrm{sub}}\}), \mathrm{Ext}(\{\mathrm{Localic}\})\}$ (`pareto_incomparable_projSub_localic`).
3. **Split-Polarity Transport Law for Conversions & Spans:**
