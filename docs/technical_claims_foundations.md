### 2.2 Canonical Foundational Grounding Categories & On-the-Nose Dual-Founding Convention
All result, remark, and inline prose claims (Part I, Part II `remark`s, and Part III) are classified strictly into one of the **Five Canonical Result Grounding Categories** (enforcing the monograph's Two-Tier Blueprint Standard across Parts I–II):
1. **`Proved by Lean code`:** Complete theorem/proposition statement and conclusion mechanized and machine-checked in Lean 4 within `HomotopyCRM/`, passing the 4-Layer Definition Check Gate with 0 `sorry`, 0 custom `axiom`, and 0 `opaque` (strict Tier-1 `\leanok` nodes on formal monograph results).
2. **`External theorem`:** Established result proved in peer-reviewed external literature or cited 2013–2026 preprints, verified against `references.bib` with exact theorem/section/page locators matched to the mathematical purpose referenced.
3. **`Folklore`:** Established category-, topos-, or locale-theoretic structural equivalence in standard mathematical currency with exact handbook/textbook references.
4. **`English language proof in monograph`:** Complete proof, explicit counterexample construction, or algebraic derivation authored in English prose inside `docs/categorical_reverse_mathematics.tex` or `docs/ch07_sets_residuum.tex`.
5. **`Unfounded`:** Stated open conjecture (`conjecture` environment or unproved core/table assertion) awaiting proof or refutation.

Additionally, Part II `definition` and `problem` environments carry exact structural groundings (`Authorial framework definition`, `Standard definition (external)`, `Open problem`). Bare integer `Line` locators denote `docs/categorical_reverse_mathematics.tex`, while `ch07:<line>` locators denote `docs/ch07_sets_residuum.tex`.

> **On-the-Nose Dual-Founding Rule:** Whenever a claim rests on multiple foundations (e.g., an external theorem paired with a monograph proof/adaptation, a Lean-verified theorem resolving or refining an external question, or an English monograph proof built over classic literature results), its primary category reflects its decisive verification tier in the monograph and `Grounding Evidence / Location` records **every contributing source with exact, purpose-matched theorem numbers, sections, and verified page ranges** plus exact monograph lines and all relevant `HomotopyCRM.*` Lean declarations.

### 2.3 Mathematical Characterization Taxonomy
- `Duality`: Order-theoretic, Heyting–Boolean, Stone/locale, or site–topos duality.

- `Model-Theoretic Amalgamation`: Fraïssé $\mathrm{AP}$/$\mathrm{SAP}$, flow-convexity, cubical $n$-$\mathrm{DAP}$, or Galois stability.
- `Postnikov / Coinductive Completeness`: Postnikov tower convergence, $\varprojlim^1$-vanishing, ordinal-rank stabilization, or coinductive witness streams.
- `Counterexample / Disproof`: Explicit countermodel separating doctrine classes or refuting a conjecture (e.g., $\mathcal{Q}$ refuting Conjecture C1).
- `Open Conjecture` / `Open Problem`: Unproven hypothesis or research horizon.
- `Metamathematical Principle`: Structural framework law, four-family admissibility, or Lean verification gate.
- `Topological Separation`: Dense orbits blocking $T_1$ separators, Borel complexity reductions, or operator-algebraic dichotomies.
