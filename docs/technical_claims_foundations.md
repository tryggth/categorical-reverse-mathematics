- **Axis I (Choice / Splitting):** Epimorphism splitting, projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$, $\mathrm{IAC} \Rightarrow \mathrm{AC}$, $\mathrm{COSHEP}$, Small Violations of Choice ($\mathrm{SVC}_{\mathrm{ret}}$), truncated higher choice ($\mathrm{AC}_n \Rightarrow \mathrm{AC}_\infty$). - **Axis III (Arithmetic Infinity / NNO):** Natural Numbers Object ($\mathrm{NNO}$), hereditarily finite sets ($\mathbf{FinSet}$), profinite lasso initial-algebra phantom ($\widehat{\mathbb{N}}_{\mathrm{lasso}} \twoheadrightarrow \widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$), Dedekind vs. Kuratowski finiteness, and biproduct tail cokernels ($\prod_{\mathbb{N}} A / \bigoplus_{\mathbb{N}} A$). Anchor witness: step-product $\mathcal{Q}$ on $[1]=\{0<1\}$ (shared with Axis V).
- **Axis IV (Characteristic / $p$-Torsion / Geometric Structure):** $p$-torsion Joyal $\delta$-rings, Nygaard filtration separation, Double-Solvent exotic smoothings, Fraïssé Strong Amalgamation ($\mathrm{SAP}$), and directed cubical $n$-amalgamation ($n=2$ vs. $n\ge 3$). Anchor witnesses: $R_2 = \mathbb{Z}[\eta]/(2\eta,\eta^2)$ with `DualInt = ℤ[ε]/(ε²)`, and facet-cycle complex $F_3 = \partial\Delta^2$ / $F_n$.
- **Axis V (Directional Invertibility / Higher Category Theory):** Weak $(\infty,\infty)$-categories, Ozornova–Rovelli–Walde four-tier ladder ($\mathrm{Cat}^L \subsetneq \mathrm{Cat}^\omega \subsetneq \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{coind}} \subsetneq \mathrm{Cat}^R$), ordinal-rank completeness, SCC trapping, $\mathrm{CatSp}$ directed stabilization, and Postnikov phantom $\mathcal{Q}$. Anchor witness: Cantor smooth-end quotient $\mathcal{Q}_{E_0} = 2^{\mathbb{N}}/E_0$ & $\mathrm{UHF}(2^\infty)$ (spanning Axes I, III, IV, V).
- **Cross-Axis / Metamathematical:** Base–Fiber Conservation across geometric morphisms $\gamma:\mathcal{E}\to\mathcal{S}$, Grothendieck–Leray 5-term conservation, and Doctrinal Comparison Triples $\mathfrak{T} \in \mathrm{Dom}(\mathrm{Res})$.

### 2.2 Canonical Foundational Grounding Categories & On-the-Nose Dual-Founding Convention
All result, remark, and inline prose claims (Parts I, Part II `remark`s, and Part III) are classified strictly into one of the **Five Canonical Result Grounding Categories**:
1. **`Proved by Lean code`:** Mechanized and machine-checked in Lean 4 within `HomotopyCRM/`, passing the 4-Layer Definition Check Gate with 0 `sorry`, 0 custom `axiom`, and 0 `opaque`.
2. **`External theorem`:** Established result proved in peer-reviewed external literature or cited 2023–2026 arXiv preprints, verified against `references.bib` with exact theorem/section/page locators matched to the purpose referenced.
3. **`Folklore`:** Established category-, topos-, or locale-theoretic structural equivalence in standard mathematical currency with exact handbook/textbook references.
4. **`English language proof in monograph`:** Complete proof, explicit counterexample construction, or algebraic derivation authored in English prose inside `docs/categorical_reverse_mathematics.tex`.
5. **`Unfounded`:** Stated open conjecture (`conjecture` environment or core conjecture in prose) awaiting proof or refutation.

Additionally, Part II `definition` and `problem` environments carry exact structural groundings (`Authorial framework definition`, `Standard definition (external)`, `Open problem`).

> **On-the-Nose Dual-Founding Rule:** Whenever a claim rests on multiple foundations (e.g., an external theorem accompanied by a monograph proof/adaptation, a Lean-verified theorem refining or resolving a question from external literature, or an English monograph proof built over classic literature results), its primary category reflects its decisive verification tier in the monograph and the `Grounding Evidence / Location` column cites **every contributing source with exact, purpose-matched theorem numbers, sections, and verified page ranges** plus exact monograph line coordinates and all relevant `HomotopyCRM.*` Lean declarations.

### 2.3 Mathematical Characterization Taxonomy
- `Duality`: Order-theoretic, Heyting–Boolean, Stone/locale, or site–topos duality.
- `Categorical Equivalence`: Equivalence or adjunction between categories, doctrines, or moduli.
- `Conservation / Solvency`: Invariant conservation across geometric morphisms, Leray 5-term partitions, or Double-Solvent collapses.
- `Choice Calibration`: Splitting of internal vs. external epimorphisms, external projectivity of $1_{\mathcal{E}}$, or higher homotopy truncation.
- `Inhabitation & Finiteness`: Non-emptiness, Dedekind vs. Kuratowski finiteness, profinite lassos, or tail cokernels.
- `Torsion Obstruction`: $p$-torsion lift torsors ($R_2$), Nygaard filtration, or nilpotent/homotopy torsion.
- `Cohomological / Spectral Obstruction`: Non-vanishing $H^1(\mathcal{E}; G)$, Giraud gerbes $H^2_{\mathrm{gerbe}}$, Postnikov $k$-invariants $H^{n+2}$, or $\mathrm{Ext}^1_{\mathrm{CondAb}}$ / $\varprojlim^1$.
- `Homotopy Type / Classifying Space`: Object classifiers $\mathcal{U}$, HITs, classifying spaces $BG$, or directed spectra $\mathrm{CatSp}$.
- `Model-Theoretic Amalgamation`: Fraïssé $\mathrm{AP}$/$\mathrm{SAP}$, flow-convexity, cubical $n$-$\mathrm{DAP}$, or Galois stability.
- `Postnikov / Coinductive Completeness`: Postnikov tower convergence, $\varprojlim^1$-vanishing, ordinal-rank stabilization, or coinductive witness streams.
- `Counterexample / Disproof`: Explicit countermodel separating doctrine classes or refuting a conjecture (e.g., $\mathcal{Q}$ refuting Conjecture C1).
- `Open Conjecture` / `Open Problem`: Unproven hypothesis or research horizon.
- `Metamathematical Principle`: Structural framework law or comparison-triple foundation.
- `Topological Separation`: Dense orbits blocking $T_1$ separators, Borel complexity reductions, or operator-algebraic dichotomies.
- `Framework Definition`: Core authorial or standard categorical definition.
